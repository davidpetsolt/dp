      How it holds together

      One retinoid at a time

      Brightening Supreme already contains retinol, and you wear it every morning. Retinal drops to two nights so you are not on a retinoid seven days a week. That was the tradeoff for making Brightening Supreme the centrepiece.

      Five recovery nights

      Dry skin needs more repair time than treatment time. Hydration and pigment work happen on the quiet nights, which is also where Mela B3 now lives so it never stacks on top of the morning retinol.

      Hyaluronic acid needs a lid

      Hyalu B5 goes on damp skin and always gets sealed with Emulsion Supreme. Left bare in dry indoor air it pulls water out of the skin instead of holding it in.

      Glycolic is parked, not retired

      Adding a 7% acid on top of daily retinol and two retinal nights is the fastest way to break a dry barrier. Leave it out for three weeks. If the skin is calm, try it once on a Sunday, never on a retinal night.

      Stop signs

      Tightness that moisturizer will not fix, stinging from products that used to be fine, a waxy shine, or redness that lingers. If any show up, drop to cleanser, moisturizer and SPF only for a week or two.

      What to expect

      • 2 to 3 wkBetter hydration, smoother texture, less morning tightness.
      • 4 to 6 wkFine lines softening, tone starting to even out.
      • 8 to 12 wkVisible change in wrinkles and dark spots. Judge the routine here, not before.
